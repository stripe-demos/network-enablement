# Welcome to Console

Your first few minutes

A question is all you need to begin. Use the conversation to work through an idea, and the canvas to explore what you find.

## Ask about your business

Start with something you’re curious about—revenue, payments, or a customer. Add a time period or a comparison when it helps. You can fill in the details together.

> “How has my revenue changed this month?”

## Explore what comes back

Charts, tables, and documents open in the **canvas** beside your conversation. Select a result beneath a response to open it. Tabs keep related work within reach.

As you ask questions, Console can create charts, tables, and documents for you to explore here.

## Keep the conversation going

Ask a follow-up to dig deeper, compare periods, or try another view. Mention a result with `@` when you want to focus on something specific.

For a bigger question, choose **Plan** from the `+` menu to work out a starting point together.
